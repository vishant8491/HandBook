## 1. Business Understanding Written Responses

### Question 1: What is Caprae’s Mission?
Caprae Capital's mission extends far beyond traditional financial engineering. Rather than treating acquisitions as transactional balance-sheet maneuvers, Caprae is dedicated to transforming businesses post-acquisition through strategic operational initiatives and the introduction of practical AI solutions. Caprae operates on a 7-year value creation journey, recognizing that the greatest enterprise value is built post-acquisition through modern technology, automated operations, and continuous operator support.

By pairing modern software development (such as proprietary sourcing tools like SaaSquatch Leads and LeadLens) with dedicated operational backing, Caprae helps portfolio companies make better data-driven decisions, streamline manual pipelines, scale revenue, and evolve good traditional businesses into enduring, technology-empowered market leaders.

---

### Question 2: Why do you want to work at Caprae Capital?
Working at Caprae Capital presents a rare intersection between high-conviction private equity and hands-on, high-velocity software engineering. Most financial firms rely on renting third-party SaaS subscriptions that fail to capture proprietary competitive edges. Caprae's ethos of building internal software (like SaaSquatch and customized AI workflows) reflects a true founder/operator mindset.

I am energized by environments that value character, horsepower, independent thinking, and the stamina to master one's craft. Being able to directly engineer software that powers deal sourcing, diligence, and portfolio transformation allows an engineer to see the tangible, dollar-for-dollar business impact of their code every single day.

---

### Question 3: How is Caprae Changing the ETA Space and Broader PE?
Caprae is fundamentally modernizing Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (ETA) and traditional Private Equity by replacing manual, fragmented search workflows with proprietary, AI-native software and systematic operator playbooks:

1. **Proprietary Software vs. Rented Data**: Instead of forcing searchers to rely on expensive, stale, and generic third-party data providers, Caprae equips operators with custom scraping, qualification, and lead intelligence tools that uncover high-fit proprietary deal flow before it reaches open broker auctions.
2. **Post-Acquisition Value Creation**: Traditional search funds often lack technical infrastructure post-close. Caprae bridges the gap between software capability and small-to-medium enterprise operations, infusing AI automation, modern CRM pipelines, and scalable cloud architectures into acquired companies.
3. **Founder/Operator Partnership**: By treating M&A as a multi-year collaborative journey (MaaS / SaaS models), Caprae shifts the paradigm from financial extraction to long-term digital empowerment.
